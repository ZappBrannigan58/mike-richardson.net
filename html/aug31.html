New PC Build

I have been playing video games more recently. I have always played games, but my amount of gaming seems to be somewhat cyclical. I have an xbox and I was paying for an xbox subscription, but the fact that I need to have that subscription to play games with my friends is something that bothers me. My friend Mike and I was playing Baldur's Gate 3 together around Christmas last year, and having a great time by the way. I was playing on xbox and he was playing on PC. I subscribed to xbox game pass then to play this game with him. Basically paying $10 a month to play a game. I had a pc as well, but the graphics card is about 10 years old, the processor is about 6 and the memory is about 6. With all that in mind it was just cheaper to pay for xbox game pass.

I also own an ASUS ROG Ally 7, which I purchased last year when on sale at Sam's Club. I purcahsed it because I was travelling a lot for work and wanted to have something to do in the hotel when I was not near home. It was great for that, but I did that with the understanding that I'm trading performance for conveniance. I have not been travelling nearly as much this year and was playing games on it at home. That is fine if I'm sitting on the couch, but when I'm tring to play at my desk and it's connected to my 4k tv, it just does not have the power the output to a display like that.

As a result I decided it was time to dust off my old desktop pc. I plugged it in, turned it on, and nothing. No picture. I then started troubleshooting by removing the GPU and plugging the hdmi cable directly into the motherboard. Still no video. I then removed the hard drive. No video. I removed the fans. No video. I removed the external drives. No video. I then removed the memory, and still no video. This led me to the conclusion that the motherboard was probably bad. I then tried flashing the BIOS, but that also failed. Several hours later, I decided it was time to replace the motherboard, but I didn't want to have to replace the entire system.

My plan was to just replace the motherboard with another AM4 motherboard. This would allow me to continue to use my video card, memory, and processor. I looked up the cost to replace the board and found that I could buy a new board for about $100. That would mean making a purchase online and waiting for shipping. My old system also only had a Ryzen 5 3600 as well, which is old and slower as well. Additionally processors have changed a lot since 2021 when I upgraded my motherboard and processor. I saw on MicroCenter that I could get a new motherboard, 16gb of dual channel memory and a new Ryzen 5 5500 processor for $249.99. This would be an improvment for the processor and fix my motherboard issue while I was at it. Plus I have always wanted to go to MicroCenter. It's like a Nerd Mecca!

While I was there though Katie asked me what I wanted vs, the minimum I needed to get my pc back up and running. If money was no object I would have gone all out, but that's not the reality of my situation. They had another bundle thouhg, one with an AM5 processor, motherboard, and that supported DDR5. I was trying to avoid DDR5 because of the current cost. Most PC parts are at an all time high due to the fact that AI is gobbling up all the resources in the world. They aren't using the same procossors and memory as my home PC, but manufacturers have shifted focus to support that boom only.

So by twisting my arm, she talked me into an upgrade. I went with a Ryzen 5 7500X3D for the processor an ASRock B8509M-C and with a G.Skill Flare X5 Series 16GB DDR5-6000 memory. This is 42% increase on the speed of the processor, which I can definitely feel. She also tried talking me into purchasing a new video card at the time. I didn't want to, because I always feel guilty spending that kind of money on myself. Katie can always use the PC as well, but lets be honest. She wont really. Plus I had a video card, it was old, but it still output video decently.

So we get home and it was late so I didn't start the build. Then the next day I went to my friends house to play One Page Rules (more on that later). When I finally got home again and was able to put my whole set together again I found out some bad news. My video card, which I tried to salvage from my old system was dead.

I wasn't sure at first, I connected everything and booted the pc up and everything lit up, but nothing happened. I moved the HDMI cable to another port on the card, still no video. I then plugged it directly into the motherboard and finally, I had something. I knew I would have to buy a new card though.

I did some searching and found that Best Buy had a RX7600 and I had a $55 off coupon. I checked what that meant for my fps in the games I play, and it too was obviously an upgrade so I purchased it. Unfortunately at this time it was like 10 PM so I had to wait another day.

Long story short I now have a fully functioning pc, that ran way better than my old one and looks great!,

4K Armies in One Page Rules

My friend Mike and I have been playing One Page Rules for some time, and I have talked about it before. I can talk about this particular subject for a very long time so I won't go into too much detail. We decided, or rather, he decided, that we should play armies that are 4k in size. Normally when we play Grim Dark Future we use a 3k point army. This allows us to add more units obviously, but also enabled me to print a gigantic 1000 point dread titan that is basically a t-rex with rockets, as you can see in the photo.

Unfortunately I ran into 2 problems with 4k armies:

  1. 3 of us played and one game took forever!
  2. My Giant T-Rex took up a gigantic amount of space!

Katie will often play with us and in this case she played my dwarven army, the only one that is painted, at least mostly! When she does we do a everyone vs everyone scenario. This is to keep it from being 1 sided, and also so we can all play together. We started our game before 11 AM on Saturday, but we did not finish until well after 5 PM. That was just 1 game!

We don't get to play a ton, mostly because of how much of a commitment it is to play, or so I suspect, so when we do I would like to play more than one game. However, after playing for over 6 almost 7 hours I just didn't have the capability to play another one. I need to talk with him again but if we do a 3v3 again I think we need to either limit it to 2.5k or 3k.

The second problem is 100% my own. I made this awesome looking dread titan, but his base is soo big that he could not fit on most of the battle field. It took me 3 turns to get him off a hill that was difficult terrain. It might have only take 2 had I used his full movement each time to get off, but I wanted to shoot as well! I should have used the whole turns to run though in retrospect, but mostly I learned I need to either scale him down 50% or spend the points on something else.
