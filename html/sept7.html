Labor Day

Today is labor day. A day as an adult that I have come to enjoy way more than I ever did as a kid. As an adult it means a day off, at least for those of us who do not work retail, for the most part, and recognition of the hard work that we put in all year. It also represents the unofficial start date for kids to go back to school!

I am sure I sound like a surly old man when I say this, but I get super excited when the kids go back to school. They're loud, they're numerous, and they are obnoxious. That doesn't mean all of them, but even the ones who are not annoy me!

Recently Katie and I were at a restaurant next to a table with some young men, I would wager between the ages of 17 - 19 or so. They kept to themselves and were not causing a scene, but they were loud and I found them obnoxious. I don't even have anything negative to say, they just minded their own business but I found them obnoxious and annoying. Katie even said something and I defended them. I can see objectively that they were fine, and I didn't feel compelled to say anything, but I don't know there is something deep inside that bothered me about the group. I really can't explain it.

I remember being that age though and being loud and obnoxious with my friends. We didn't think we were at the time, but I can tell you, we objectively were. When I think back to some of our behaviours and the things we did and I cringe. I'm sure these kids will eventually do the same thing.

You know what else I like about back to school? The back to school sales for school supplies, like pens, markers, and more importantly notebooks. I don't know what it is about notebooks, like composition notebooks, but I really like them. I have a bunch, but when they cost like $.50 or less I feel compelled to buy more! I noticed this week though that the company that produces them lowered the page count to 80, from 100. Target had these for $.39 each. But like 4 years ago they were the same price and had 100 pages. I would much rather pay $.50 and get the other 20 pages than they remove them and keep the price the same. Additionally if they're not on sale for back to school they can cost up to $5 a notebook, which I will admit all seem to be for 100 pages. At that cost though you can get notebooks with more pages, and hard letherbound cover for nearly the same cost, if you calculate the number of pages of each down to cost per page. I will admit they cost more for the notebooks, but often have more pages and it equals out to a similar cost per page.

Maybe I'm the only one who find this interesting, but again, I like notebooks!

Labor Day Plans

Unfortunately for Katie, she does work retail. That means she has to go to work today, which means that I get to do anything that I want to today. A long time ago I purchased the game Civilization VI on Steam. I'm sure it was on sale which is why i purchased it. That and I remember as a kid playing Civilization a long time ago, and I loved it!

My plan today was to play some Civilization, just a few hours or so. Playing this game is like time travel! by that I mean I sit down to play for a few minutes and BAM! 4 hours have passed.

It is both fun and frustrating because of how long the game takes. It sucks to spend 5 hours playing a game to lose in the end.

Enjoying the Fruits of my Labor!
