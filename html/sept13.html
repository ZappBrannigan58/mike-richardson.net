Sunday Thunderstorms

This has been a very wet year for Pittsburgh. I have complained about the rocks in my driveway running down the road several times, and I'm now to the point where I'm not even sure if it's worth bothering with. What if all of life is a simulation and because I keep fixing the rocks some kid somewhere playing a video game keeps making sever storms to watch my fat butt rake them up again? I think it's a similar theory to the one I posed yesterday about cost of living...

I looked it up and in fact for the year of 2026, which still has 3 months left go go we are 6 inches of the typical amount of rainfall for the year. The good news is I have a well, and its made me feel less concenred about it! I usually complain in late August as well about everyones grass dying and it looking yellow everywhere around here, but that definitely isn't going to be a problem today!

I made beef stew for dinner when the downpour became too much for our saturated ground to withstand anymore. The power went out while I was in the boiling step of the dinner. It wasn't super disruptive since I have gas for my stove, and it was already on, but it was annoying to say the least. The stew was great though!

I'm Going to Need a Sump Pump For My Basement

You probably think that this heading is directly related to that last bit, It's not really. My basement, for the most part has stayed relatively dry despite the amount of rain we have been seeing. I think this is kind of impressive seeing as how it's a lot of rain, but also I have a Well in my basement!

When we got our house inspected before purchase, the inspector made a comment about how the Washer Machine was pumping water over 6 feet up in the air into the sewer pipe. I asked him if we needed to something about it and he said it doesn't affect the inspection, but he was shocked it worked... Well newsflash... it doesn't.

We have been dealing with a situation where the washer machine shakes violently everytime it is in the spin and drain cycle. I assumed at first this was because Katie and Seth were not balancing the loads they put in the machine. I also supsected they were overfilling it, and that may have been a small part of it. Now I think the damn thing is not expelling all the water in it, and is causing it to be off balance when it spins out. If you shake it, when there is no load in the machine you can feel and hear water sloshing around. I dont know if that is normal, as I have never before needed to shake a wash machine, but I don't think it is.

I did some digging around on the internet, and by that I mean Katie did some digging around on the internet and I'm going to take all the credit and I think we found a solution. We think what we are going to do is get one of those crappy palstic/rubber sink things you see in basements all the time. We are going to hook that to the sump pump we want to buy. Then we are going to remove the rain hose from the washer machine, and drape it into the sink. We will then connect the sump pump to the same pipe the washer is connected to now, and use the existing plumbing to make it work.

We went to Lowes and did some looking around. I think we can get this whole thing fixed and set up for around $500 dollars. I'm hoping for much less. I want to get it fixed asap, and just put it on the credit card, but we just paid of our credit cards and I'm not in a hurry to build debt back up again. Either way I hope it works out for us!
