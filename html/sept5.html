Grandfather Clock Trip

When I was a kid my parents purchased a custom clock that was made in the black forest of Germany. The pendulum has their names and year it was purcahsed engraved on it and has been something in my family that everyone appreciated and wanted to inherit some day. Not just because of the value of the clock, which I'll be honest I do not know the value of, but because of the connection between my parents as well as just nostalgia and the memories that get triggered when you hear those chimes.

Since then a lot has changed. My step dad passed away in 2012, my mother moved on and has re-married. Her current husband, whom I like, is a heavy equipment operator who travels for work. They purchased a trailer and have not lived in her house for over 6 months. As a result my mother decided to let my sister move into her house and take over the mortgage and ownership once it is paid off. She also declared that the clock is now mine to eventually pass down to my son.

A few weeks ago I helped her move her things that she wanted to keep into a storage locker in WV. We put the clock in the same locker at that time with the understanding that Katie and I would be back to pick it up and take it to PA together. Well this weekend was that weekend.

My mother measured the clock prior to us putting it into the storage locker and declared it at just under 6 feet in height. So I went on to the uhaul website and booked a 10 foot truck that had a max height of 6 foot 3 inches. My plan was to rent a truck in the town of Sutton WV and make it a one way trip to PA.

U-haul called me on Friday to inform me that they did not have any 10' trucks and they would be upgrading me to a 20' one. I wasn't thrilled about the increase in cost that would happen as I am sure that a truck twice the size will cost more in gas. I was glad to still be able to make my trip though. Futher the truck location I reserved it with was not going to work either, and I would have to pick up the larger truck at a nearby town, which they gave me a few options to choose from.

I ended up choosing to pick up the larger truck from the nearby town of Burnsville which was on my way. Uhaul also gave me additional miles in my original agreement. The first truck came with 180 miles I believe, afterwhich I would have to pay an additional fee per mile. This new truck came with 220 miles. Even at 180 miles the trip should only have been 133 so I had plenty of wiggle room, but I appreciate the consideration.



So today, Saturday, We drive the 3 hours to WV and stop in Burnsville to get this truck. I lived in WV for 8 years, and drove past, through Burnsville several times so I thought I knew what to expect. I was wrong. This uhaul location was in a back alley/road in a store that I woul dhave presumed was closed and out of business. It was a Napa Auto Parts store, but it was such a random assortment of crap in it that I thought for sure it was no longer in use and was just a storage locker for some sort of hoarder.

I tried to pickup the uhaul using their website and not actually talking to anyone. Partly because I didn't want to go into the store, and there was no sign assuring me that the two were related, but also because I don't want to talk to anyone in person if I can avoid it. The last time I rented a uhaul and picked it up the whole process took like 30 minutes and the only reason I could find for it was general computer illiteracy. My phone said I had 5G connection and full bars, but if that was 5G then I'm a model.

I had to go inside the business to get things squared away. Luckily they did support the uhaul side of the business. To my surprise they were relatively quick and before I knew it i had my key to the uhaul. Unfortunatley that isn't the end of this leg of the trip. I put the key into the ignition and turned the key...nothing happened.

To the credit of the business they swapped the battery out and got me up and running. The negative though is it took over an hour to swap the battery. I don't want to be negative and unapreciative, but I would rather have installed it myself, as these people working like people who had to be there all day, whereas I was in a hurry to complete something I didn't want to do anyway.

Once I was on the road though I went to the the storage locker and put the clock on my truck. This is where I was informed by my mother that the clock was in reality just under 7ft tall. Not the 6ft height they originally told me about. This means if uhaul had not actually given me a free upgrade to a 20' truck I would not have been able to fit the clock inside it and upright.

Katie and I then made the trip back to PA with her in her car and me driving the uhaul. The truck, which was relatively new, did not have cruise control. This blows my mind, how a business exists to support people moving across the city, state, country, what have you, decides they do not need cruise control in their vehicles is monstrous. I get on paper why they would opt for that, I'm sure at scale it saves them a heap of money, but as a consumer, what a terrible decision!

We get the clock home and unload it from the truck. It works great and sounds amazing. Nothing bad happened or damage was incurred from the move, so I'm very happy with that. I pulled out from my drive way nose first since I saw Jeff back out when I moved into the house and the degree of the driveway caused the back of the truck to take a small chunk out of the road and I wanted to avoid that so I went out nose first.

What a bad idea. First I couldn't see anything to the right of my vehcile. He drive way curves slightly to the left and the road curves slightly to the right. That means I was facing down the driveway but couldn't see anything. I also couldn't hear anything because the truck was so loud that hearing any oncoming traffic was impossible. So hoping and praying, I pulled out.

The truck was too large to make the right turn onto the road so I had to back up and pull out a second time. I saw in the side mirror the whole time the tail end of the truck hit my mailbox. I could have potentially backed up again, but in doing so would have backed up into the grass hill next to my driveway so I kept easing forward moving the mailbox more and more with each inch.

I made it out and I returned my truck, but my mailbox is no longer facing straight or at a 90 degree angle. Which is frustrating because I just fixed that mailbox from when Walmart backed over it... I feel like the family on Home Alone with their statue in front of the house, only mine is a mailbox.

Dinner and Relaxing

After we returned the truck, it was later in the day than I wanted it to be. My original plan was to be done around 2 and it was closer to 5 or 6. So Katie and I relaxed a little bit on the couch before we left to go to the store.

Recently when I've been playing games at my computer or working from home I've noticed that my 8 year old computer chair is starting to hurt my butt. This is definitely more an indictment on my own physical activity because the problem really only persist if I spend more than an hour at my desk, but in the world of gaming and work from home, one hour is not a ton of time. I determined that meant it was time for a new chair.

I did some searching around and Walmart has a gaming chair they keep in stock that seems kind of nice, but it was $130 and frankly I don't care for walmart. I presume that if they are selling a "gaming" chair in the store that it's not really that good. That's my own personal bias, and is likely not correct, but I still didn't like the idea. Further the display they have of the chair is in a location where you can see it, but not sit in it. If I can't sit in the chair then I'll order one off Amazon for half that price.

Amazon was and is always an option, but I've been trying to rely on them less lately after my last spat with the filament I ordered. Additionally I really wanted to try the chair I was going to buy. My current chair was given to me by my mother for a birthday gift and it has been great and long lasting, but the padding seems to have finally broken down on it. It also cost like $300.

I have purchased office furniture in the past from Staples and got a decent deal and quality furniture so I checked their website and found a chair that looked promising. The upside was that I could sit in it and get a feel. I will say that Staples had a great selection and plenty of models to sit and and feel out.

I ended up buying my chair there in white to match the furniture we have in our house, and it is super comfortable. I spent more than I would have at walmart or amazon, but getting a feel for it first was worth every extra penny.

Afterward we tried Wing Kings, which is newly opened next to the staples around her. I really enjoyed it! Their ranch dressing is like crack! After that Katie and I relaxed watched TV, and then went to bed.

Go Away, I'm sleeping!
