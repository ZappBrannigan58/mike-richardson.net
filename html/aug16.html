Sunday - Grocery Shopping

I don't know how it happened. For a few years Katie and I used to go grocery shopping on Friday afternoons after work. This had it's ups and downs. The upside was that we had the weekend to do whatever else we wanted to do. That may be other chores and things as well, but it didn't mean making a menu, going to the store, and then putting it all away. The downside though is that we spend the entire day Friday doing things we don't necessarily want to do what with work followed by shopping. This may be a more us problem than others since we shop around and don't just go to one location. Normally we hit up Sam's Club, Aldi, and then Walmart.

Recently we went to a Trader Joe's though and while overall I don't get it. They do have some pretty nice things there. For instance I eat a chicken salad for lunch each day at work and I like to put blue cheese/gorganzola on it. I usually get a 5oz container from Aldi, but for only a $1 more Trader Joe's had an 8oz container! I love blue cheese so I was pretty excited. Now I worry I'll have to make a weekly trip to Trader Joe's as well.

Here lately though we have been going out Sunday morning and getting our groceries. This also has it's ups and downs. The upside is that we don't have to go out and lose an entire Friday. The downside, I lose half of my Sunday. This is partly because Aldi, and Sam's Club do not open until 9am. I recognize that I am an early bird, my wife and I both are, but 9am is kind of late for grocery stores to open right?

After that I feel like we are racing the church crowd. I don't want to get stuck out grocery shopping after the elderly leave church, it just creates confusion and frustration for me.

Hobby Fun!

I don't have any expectations or plans for it, but I would like to do this more for me and for fun. I'm not sure what that will look like yet, and I don't have an outline for this game yet, but I am working toward that. Right now I'm studying and learning JavaScript.

I chose JavaScript for the obvious reason, which is this site as well as my other sites. I don't have a specific need for it, but I think it makes for a more natural transition to more extensive levels of programming. Maybe I've gotten better, or more used to HTML and CSS, but I feel like they're not full programming languages and it seems to me like anyone should be able to pick it up. I'm hoping that by learning and then implementing JavaScript into my sites I can learn some more advanced concepts of Coding that are not represented in CSS and HTML and lay some good ground work for other languages.
