Burn Out!

I believe I have spoken about the divestiture the company I work for has been going through for a little bit of time. We had a scenario where everyone in IT was working long hours as fast and as hard as possible for a long time, like over a year. We did it too, everyone did a great job and we accomplished our goals. Nowe I am facing a different challenge and that is... burnout.

Things have started to get back to normal, at least pre-divestiture normal (knock on wood) and I think that is a good thing. The problem is the damage the craziness and stress had on the team is evident in everything we do. Everyone appears to be burned out and everyone appears to be over it, to be candid. I myself am struggling to deal with the day to day type of task we normally deal with. I know they're still important, but the scale of the work is so dramatically decreased that I find myself struggling to remain engaged.

This may sound like I miss the hectic and breakneck speed of things that were happening during the divestiture, but believe me I do not, but there are lingering negative impacts for sure. It's almost like PTSD, in that you get so used to being stressed and on edge, that you struggle to go back to something else. I know it's not that extreme, its just an office job after all, but I don't know how else to explain it.

Even though yesterday was a holiday, and I had a paid time off, it just wasn't enough for me to get back into the normal groove. This is likely also due to the fact that I have only taken a single week off this year. Katie and I have plans to take the first week of October and then I also will take the last week and a half around Christmas.

Turn Me Over, I'm Done
