Going to the Mall

Today I hung out at the house with Katie almost all day. We sat and watched Ted Lasso from like 8 until 12 or so. I decided then I wanted to buy these turtle chips they sell at a store there. They also sell them at 5 Below, but they don't have the steak and Wasabi flavor that the mall has. They're delicious!

While we were there we stopped in HotTopic, just on a whim and found a "The Mummy" hoodie with Brenden Fraser on it. Katie loves that movie, it's her favorite. The front had Brendan Fraser with a sword, and the back says "Death is only the beginning". On the sleeves are pictures of the lock to the book of the dead. She saw it and immediatly wanted it.

Luckily they had one hoodie in her size. At first she thought the sleeves were too long, until I pointed out that they were the types of sleeves with thumb holes in them. Meaning they were supposed to be long intentionally. After that she became even more excited about it.


Out of My Lane

I didn't go to college until I was 25. I think everyone I know knows that, because I discuss it a lot. Its not something I'm proud of per say, but I think it's more uncommon for someone to skip college for a while and then actually go back and change their professional trajectory. It was a lot of work, and was a lot of stress, but I would definitely do it all over again.

With that said, I think alot of people who work in a professional environment occassionally gets the feeling that they are unqualified, or feel out of place. That is not what I'm talking about. I am pretty good at my job, I may not be the best, but I do a good job and I think my sociability is a strong asset in my field.

What I'm talking about is what if I'm out of my lane? I make a decent wage and I worked hard for it, but everytime I feel like I get a raise I'm not making anymore headway. I keep treading water with each raise, and my quality of life and spending money never seem to go up. I realize some of that may be due to lifestyle creep. I know a lot of people in my career and business who live paycheck to paycheck. Its lead me to a wierd conspiracy theory...

What if the reason that inflation seems out of control, that gas prices are untenable, and that everything seems to be going in the wrong direction, is all because I went back to school to do better for myself and my family? I'm not truly egotistical to think this is all on me, but people like me. What if the reason these things are getting so bad is because of us? Maybe we aren't supposed to be happy and have the same quality of life as other... Crazy I know...
