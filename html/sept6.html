Light Bulb for Clock

Lately Katie and I have been spending our Sunday mornings grocery shopping. Our goal, typically is to be completley done by the time the church crowd gets out of church and things start getting crowded. I'm conflicted on this process, because I would prefer not to spend my Sunday grocery shopping. The way we used to do it was to go out on Friday afternoon to grocery shop and that means that on Friday we don't get time to do things we want to do, we end up going to work and then shopping and then bad. I guess it's fine.

Things took a little longer today than I would normally like because Katie and I tried to see if we could find a light bulb for our grandfather clock. We checked with google and Katie thought she found the size we needed and wanted to stop by Do-It-Best to see if we could find. A single bulb was $10 a piece. Which I thought was high, so we decided to check at Lowes. The result is that the bulbs are just kind of expensive for what they are. We thought we had the right bulb, but we did not.

I found a receipt inside the clock and a metal plate that said it was purchased from house of 1000 clocks in Triberg Germany, but when I checked their website they only seem to have cuckoo clocks for sale now, so I couldn't find the grandfather clock we have to see if I could find the real size.

We then returned the bulb and tried again, and still couldn't find the right size. I think we are going to try one more, but if that doesn't work we'll probably just upgrade to a new option, maybe one that is motion activated.

Grilling and Relaxing

The nice thing about going grocery shopping on a Sunday is that I was able to buy some nice thick New York Strip steaks. I put them on the grill with some fresh carrots and potatoes. I have done this many times with potatoes and they make for great baked potatoes. This was my first time doing so with carrots and I wouldn't do it again. They didn't really work out like I had hoped.

My mother also gave me some chocolate Crown Royal whiskey when I was in WV picking up my clock. It seemed to me like mixing that with peanut butter whiskey would turn the drink into a reese cup drink. It did not. Still it was a nice and relaxing time and the steaks were delicious.

Good and Sauced!
