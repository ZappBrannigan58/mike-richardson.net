Thank You Friday!

I think Friday may be the greatest day of the week. Yes, it's not a day off, typically, but it is hope. That sounds dramatic, but thats the same thing that makes Christmas Eve so amazing and powerful. Its the hope of what possibilites exist. It's like the Schrödinger’s cat of week days!

I assume that everyone knows what that means, but in case you don't; Schrödinger’s cat is a thought expirment. The basic concerpt is if you put a cat in a box with a dangerous substance, like a dangerous poison in a vial. When you put them both in the box they are both seperate and the cat is fine. But once the box is closed you don't know what happened with the cat and the poison so in effect the cat is both Dead and Alive. It's a great alegory to explain quantum superposition on a very basic level

This is Friday because it's the weekend. You may have plans, you may not. The weekend can go one way and be great, or in some cases not so much, but it's wide open and you never know for sure which one it is going to be. This is applicable in a lesser extent, at least in my opinion, for every other day of the week, but a large portion of the normal work-week is taken up by work, and even in an environment where the day to day changes a lot, its still pretty stable.

I have worked jobs in the past where I had to work weekends, or holidays so I know that this isn't 100% applicable to everyone. I feel for them, I do, but the eve before a day off or a few days off still is applicable as well. It's not specific to Friday, but Friday is the more recognizable and understandable translation to the idea.

Maybe that's silly, or maybe I'm just lazy. I think most people though, do enjoy their time off, and I think they can relate at least to some extent.

I Love Steak!

My wife, son, and I decided to go to Texas Roadhouse together. As far as steak houses go, at least chain ones, Texas Roadhouse is my favorite one. I really think you get a great deal and value there, plus as the title states - "I Love Steak!"

Katie had a gift card she recieved for her birthday so she had plans for a really good meal. We ordered fried pickles as an apatizer and Katie ordered a frozen margarita. I think she got the Hurricane Margarita, I could be wrong. That one drink got her pretty tipsy though!

What makes me think they're such a great value is I ordered a 16oz sirloin with fries, and a side salad; all of which was only $25. That's not nothing, but a full pound of steak with two sides for that price I think is a great value. This also comes with free bread, and peanuts!

Katie ordered a 6oz sirloin and ribs combo. She got it with mashed potatoes and corn. She mixes them together and I think it's gross, but ya know do what you wanna do I guess.

Seth ordered mushroom chicken with mashed potatoes and gravy and a cup of chili. He got the chili with onions and cheese, which I thought was weird. Seth has never liked onions, I know because I'm the only one in the family who does and often have to add them to things on the side since no one else likes them.

Full of Steak and Happy!
