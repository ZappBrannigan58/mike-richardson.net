Poison Ivy

Katie told me on Friday that she thinks she had a bug bite on her forehead. She was hanging out with her sister on Thursday afternoon and they were doing some planting in her garden. She assumed she got bitten by some bug. I thought that a bug bite in the middle of your forehead was a little wierd, as I have never experienced a bug bite on my forehead before, but I thought she would know.

Well this morning she was telling me that she has bug bits all down her neck as well as on her hands and hip as well. I asked her if she thought it was poison ivy as we did yardwork earlier on Tuesday and she ran the weed eater while I did the rest of the mowing. She was super sure that I was wrong and it was not poison Ivy.

After that we had to run some errands and stopped by Target to get her some anti-itch cream, since it was so itchy. I don't know what I expected here, but the location of anti-itch cream at stores doesn't really make a lot of sense to me. I found them next to the patches for sore muscles. I'm not sure why and I found it by accident when I lost Katie.

Basically after that Katie was soo itchy and uncomfortable that we kind of just hung out the rest of the day. We watched a lot of Ted Lasso, and I have some feelings about that and Maybe I'll get into that later. Te point being that we really didn't do anything. I knew that today, Sunday, we would go out and grocery shop for the week.

That night at around like 3 or 4 AM she got out of bed and went downstairs to the living room. I know this because I rolled over and he was gone, I assumed in the bathroom. She was not. Apparently she was so itchy it kept her up at night and she was worried she would keep me up as well.

I went downstairs to check on her at 6:30 and she told me that she thinks "She might have poision Ivy, or an allergic reaction to some peppermint oil she had put on her at a spa. I really didn't think it was an allergic reaction to peppermint oil, since it had lasted several days, several showers, and was delayed. She didn't get an immediate reaction. I don't have any evidence that this was only postulated out of stubborness to admit that I was right, but it certainly felt that way. She is sensitive to poison Ivy. She has been exposed to it a few times since we have been together and in every case she has to get steroids to get over it, so I kind of know what I'm looking at.

She decided that she needed to go to the hospital. So we went to the ER. I suggested we go to a med express, but she had a bad experience with a doctor at a med express and now doesn't want to go.

I bet you will never guess what they said...

Poison Ivy

Yes, so I was right all along. They prescribed her with some steroids and sent us on our way.

Grocery Shopping on Football Sunday

It was close to noon by the time we made it to Sam's Club to start grocery shopping for the week. Everywhere we went was a mad house and so full of people. I don't normally mind a lot of people in one place, although I minded it a lot less when I was younger, but I find myself not having patience for people with no situational awareness. It seems that on busy days the amount of poeple who are completely oblivous to those around them just goes through the roof.

We went to Aldi, and the lines to check out when down the aisles and around the check out. I suspect this is due to the normal after church crowd when grocery shoppin on Sundays, which I try to avoid, but the Steelers game I think also played a part. Typically the last minute rush of people at the grocery store prior to a game is pretty ridiculous. It's obviously not superbowl busy, but Pittsburghers take their football games serious!s

Afterward Katie watched the game while falling asleep on the couch. I did some coding course work on udemy.com and played some video games while she watched the game. After a nap and the steroids starting to kick in the swelling and itching on her poison ivy started to go down.

Itchy by Association!
